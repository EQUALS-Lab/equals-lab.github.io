About the PI

Asst. Prof. Vini Chaudhary

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Mississippi State University and the Director of the intElligent QUantum And wireLess communication Systems (EQUALS) Lab. My research interests include quantum communication networks & computing, wireless communication networks, spectrum sharing, cybersecurity, cyber-physical systems / smart IoT, and explainable AI for 5G/6G communications.
