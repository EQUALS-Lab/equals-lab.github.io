Asst. Prof. Vini Chaudhary

Lab Director - Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Engineering

Vini Chaudhary received the Ph.D. degree in electrical engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi, India, in 2021, and the M.Tech. degree in electrical engineering from IIT Kanpur, India, in 2016. She is currently an Assistant Professor with the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Mississippi State University, USA. Prior to this, she was a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Northeastern University, USA, from 2021 to 2024. Her research interests include AI/ML/XAI-enabled technologies for wireless communications, quantum communication network protocols, quantum computing for large-scale wireless network design, multimodal data fusion with footprint reduction, and signal processing.

Research areas: Quantum computing for communications, AI for wireless networks, 6G architectures.
Positions: Assistant Professor, Department of Computer Science and Engineering; Director, EQUALS.
